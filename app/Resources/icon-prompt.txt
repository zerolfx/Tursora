Current export contract (2026-09-12):
The historical prompts below describe how the original artwork was created; they are not instructions for the delivered PNG or ICNS. In particular, the statement that the system will apply outside corner masking was an incorrect assumption for Tursora's manually packaged ICNS and is superseded.

AppIcon-artwork.png retains the original square artwork after the historical foreground-spacing edit. render-icon.swift draws that artwork at its original scale and position on a transparent 1024x1024 canvas, clipped to exactly one rounded rectangle: x=80, y=80, width=864, height=864, cornerWidth=192, cornerHeight=192. The tile ends at x=944 and y=944. The exterior and corners are transparent with an antialiased boundary. No new stroke, shadow, nested tile or foreground redraw is added. This is Tursora's chosen export geometry, not an assertion about Apple's exact system mask. make-icon.sh regenerates AppIcon.png and the 16-1024px ICNS family; make-app.sh invokes it before packaging.

Two built-in image_gen edge-edit attempts were rejected because their alpha fringes were unsuitable. Neither result ships. The delivered assets use the retained original artwork and deterministic CoreGraphics clipping.
